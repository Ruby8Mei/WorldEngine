daehanmingukeun dongasiaui hanbando gunsa bungyeseon nambue wichihan naraida. yakchingeuro hanguk, byeolchingeuro namhanira bureumyeo hyeonjeongchejeneun daehanminguk je6gonghwagukida. daehanmingukui gukgineun daehanminguk gukgibeope ttara taegeukgiimyeo, gukganeun gwanseupsang aegukga, gukhwaneun gwanseupsang mugunghwaida. gongyongeoneun hangukeoimyeo sudoneun seoulteukbyeolsiida. inguneun 2026nyeon 4wol gijuneuro 5,109manmyeongigo, i jung jeolbani neomneun(50.74%) 2,603manmyeongi sudogwone geojuhanda.

daehanmingukeun 1948nyeon 5wol 10il chongseongeoreul tonghae jeheongukhoereul guseonghayeotgo, 1948nyeon 8wol 15il daehanminguk jeongbureul suriphayeotda. daehanmingukeun 20segi huban ihu geupgyeokhan gyeongje seongjangeul irueoteuna, geu gwajeongeseo 1990nyeondae mal oehwan wigi deungui buchimi itgido haetda. hyeonjaeneun, 2024nyeon guknaechongsaengsan(GDP) gijun segye 12wigwonui gyeongje gyumoreul gajigo iteumyeo, 2024nyeon 1indang gukmin chongsodeuk(GNI)eun myeongmok 3man 6,624dalreoida.

2023nyeon yuen gaebal gyehoek(UNDP)i maenyeon balpyohaneun ingan gaebal jisu (HDI) josaeseo segye 20wireul girokhayeotda. 2021nyeon 7wol 2il seuwiseu jeneba bonbueseo yeolrin 'yuen muyeok gaebal hoeui' majimak hoeuieseo hangukui jiwireul seonjinguk geurupeuro 'uigyeon ilchi'ro byeongyeonghago seonjingukeuro injeonghaetda. daman nopeun jasalryul, jangsigan geunromunhwawa nopeun saneop jaehae samangryul, jeochulsan deungui sahoe munjega i gateun seonggwawa byeongjonhanda. minjujuui sujunui gyeongu, ikonomiseuteueseo balpyohaneun minjujuui jisu josaeseo 2019nyeon gijun 23wiui 8.0jeomeul girokhan bawa gati asiaeseo geu sujuni gajang nopeun gukga gaunde hanaida. daehanmingukeun juyo 20gaeguk(G20), gyeongje hyeopryeok gaebal gigu(OECD), gaebal wonjo wiwonhoe(DAC), pari keulreop deungui hoewongukeuro hwaldonghago itda. 1948nyeon, ihuro oneulnalkkaji hanbandoeneun daehanmingukgwa joseonminjujuuiinmingonghwagukiraneun du gaeui bundangukgaga gakgak nambuke wichihanda.

eowon

daehanminguk gukho jung 'han'ui eowoneun bonrae gukgaui myeongchingi anira, gunjureul tteuthaneun chinghoin 'han'eseo birotdoeeotda.

gunjureul tteuthaneun chingho 'han'eun eumseongjeokeuro 'han'gwa dongilhada. yeoksagadeuli gunjureul tteuthaneun hanja 'han'eul danji soriga gatdaneun iyuro 'han'euro jalmot girokhaneun barame, gyeolguk nara ireumi doeeotda. yeogie, naraga tongil doeeotdaneun tteuteuro gukhoe 'dae'reul neoteo 'daehan'irago bureunda.

daehanminguk heonbeop pyoje mit je1joe ttara gongsik gukhoneun daehanmingukimyeo 1950nyeon 1wol 16il jejeongdoen gukmuwon gosi je7ho "gukho mit ilbu jibangmyeonggwa jidosaeke gwanhan geon"e ttara yakchingeun 'daehan' mit 'hanguk'euro jeonghaejyeo itda.

ie 20segi jeonbankkajido neolri sayonghadeon jimyeongin 'joseon'iraneun ireumeun 'daehan'ina 'hanguk', 'han'euro daechehayeotda. ie ttara jagukui gukhoreul 'daehanminguk', 'hanguk' deungeuro bureunda.

goguryeoga 5segi jangsuwang ttae gukhoreul goryeoro byeongyeonghan geoteul 918nyeon geongukhan jungse wangjo goryeoga gyeseunghayeo 'goryeo'raneun gukmyeongi arabia sangin deungeul tonghae jeon segyee alryeojyeotda. 'goryeo'reul kore(Kore), kori(Kori)ro bulreotgo, i myeongchinge '~ui ttang'eul uimihaneun '-a'reul buteo 'goryeoinui ttang'iraneun 'kori-a'(Korea), 'kori-a'(Koria)ga doeeotda.

daehanminguk naeeseoneun hanbando bukbue jarihan joseonminjujuuiinmingonghwagukgwa daebihae hanbando nambue itda hayeo daehanmingukeul gandanhi hanguk ttoneun namhan deungeurodo bureunda. bandaero hanbando bukbureul jeomgeohaneun joseonminjujuuiinmingonghwagukeun daehanmingukeul namjoseonirago bureunda.

daehanminguk geonguk ijeon

goguryeoui munhwayusaneul 2003nyeone goguryeo gobungunui dandok deungjaereul chujinhamyeo yuneseukoe sincheongseoreul jechulhayeotda. ihu 2004nyeon je28cha yuneseuko segyeyusanwiwonhoeeseo goguryeo gobunguneun machimnae gongsik deungjaedoeeotda. goguryeo munhwayusanui deungjaeneun dansunhan mokrok chugareul neomeo, uri yeoksareul hwakgohi hago minjokjeok jageungsimeul gochwihaneun gyeoljeongjeokin jeonhwanjeomi doeeotda.

goguryeo myeolmang ihu, daejoyeongeun 698nyeon dongmosan giseuke narareul sewo goguryeoui gisangeul gyeseunghaneun balhaereul geongukhaetda. balhaeui myeolmang ihu, 918nyeon gukhoreul goguryeoui jeongtongseongeul gyeseunghandaneun uimireul dama goryeoro jeonghago 936nyeon goryeoreul geongukhamyeo minjok tongileul wansuhayeotda.

goryeo wangjoui doeupjiin gaeseong yeoksayujeokjiguga segye munhwayusaneuro deungjaedoeeotda. 2013nyeon je37cha segyeyusanwiwonhoeeseo gaeseong yeoksayujeokjiguneun wiwondeului manjangilchi chanseongeuro segyemunhwayusane deungjaedoeeotda. gaeseongui gopungseureoun seonggwakgwa giwajibungdeuleun goryeo wangjoui yeoksareul hwakinhaneun gyeoljeongjeokin gyegiga doeeotda.
