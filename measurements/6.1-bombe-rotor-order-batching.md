# Historic Bombe rotor-order batching

Date: 2026-09-14

## Scope

This stage batches the existing historic diagonal-board engine across the
approved Legacy rotor pool I, II, III, IV, and V. It does not complete partial
stecker assignments and does not add Naval Enigma or INOP-38 behavior.

## Fixed work definition

- Traversal follows pool order at each rotor slot and rejects repeated rotors.
- The first orders are I II III, I II IV, and I II V.
- The last orders are V IV I, V IV II, and V IV III.
- There are 60 distinct orders and 17,576 core positions per order.
- Total estimated work is 1,054,560 core positions.
- Retention bounds are 16 stops per order and 64 stops for the whole batch.
- Stop counting continues after either retention bound is reached.

## Representative measurements

The `cli-werror` build ran on the local Windows host with synthetic public
fixtures.

| Fixture | Orders | Positions | Counted stops | Time |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Public steckered demonstration | 60 | 1,054,560 | 14 | 30.517 s |
| Public no-result menu | 60 | 1,054,560 | 0 | 31.695 s |

The no-result menu used crib `ABCDABCDABCD` and ciphertext
`BCDABCDABCDA`. Both are synthetic public fixture text.

## Correctness evidence

The dedicated self checks verify exact one-order equivalence with the existing
single-order engine, deterministic complete enumeration, cancellation between
orders, cancellation within an order, a batched no-result result, and stop
counting beyond both retention bounds. The complete fixture also checks all 60
order summaries against the approved traversal.
